GestureVerse

Draw in the air. Turn it into an object. Grab it, lift it, throw it.

  1. Allow the camera. Nothing leaves your device — tracking runs locally in your browser.
  2. Position your hand. Hold it about an arm's length away, palm facing the camera, whole hand in frame.
  3. Wait for the cursor. A glowing dot follows your index fingertip.
  4. Draw a circle. Point with your index finger, then pinch to grab what you made.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop with decent light.
