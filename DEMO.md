# Demo workflow

A scripted walkthrough of the signature experience, for a booth, a family day, or a first
run-through. It takes about two minutes end to end.

> **Before the doors open:** run `npm run serve`, open the page, allow the camera, and leave the
> onboarding screen up. Check the lighting. Turn on **Full screen** and press **H** to hide the
> panels for a clean public demo. If there will be children, switch **Allow throwing** off.

---

## 0. Setup (5 seconds)

Position the visitor about an arm's length from the camera, palm towards it, whole hand in frame.
Point at the screen with the index finger and wait for the glowing cursor to appear.

**Say:** *"That dot is your fingertip. You're holding the pen."*

---

## 1. Draw in the air (20 seconds)

With your index finger, trace a slow circle in the air. A neon trail follows you.

Then **open your hand**. The stroke ends cleanly — no connecting line.

**Say:** *"That's not a drawing any more."*

---

## 2. Draw it into an object (10 seconds)

The circle is recognised and pops into a glowing 3D sphere, with a particle burst.

**Say:** *"I drew a circle. It became a sphere. Anything I draw becomes something I can hold."*

**Try:** draw a square (→ cube), a triangle (→ cone), a star (→ star). Freehand lines become
glowing tubes.

---

## 3. Grab it (15 seconds)

Move your cursor onto the sphere. A selection outline appears.

Now **pinch your thumb and index finger together**. The sphere attaches to your hand — no jump,
because the grab preserves the offset.

**Say:** *"Pinch to pick it up. Watch — it doesn't teleport into my hand, it stays exactly where I
grabbed it."*

---

## 4. Lift, carry and place (20 seconds)

Keep pinching and move your hand up, then left, then right. The sphere follows smoothly, lagging
just enough to feel physical.

Bring your hand **towards and away from the camera** — the object moves in depth.

**Release the pinch.** The sphere is placed, with a spark burst. Move your hand away. It stays.

**Say:** *"Let go and it stays put. It's a real object in a real space now."*

---

## 5. Grab it again (10 seconds)

Move back onto the sphere, pinch again, carry it somewhere else. It works every time.

**Say:** *"And I can do that as many times as I like."*

---

## 6. Rotate and resize (20 seconds)

Hold the object with one hand. Bring your **second hand** into frame.

- **Spread your hands apart** → the object grows. Bring them together → it shrinks.
- **Twist your two hands** → the object rotates.

**Say:** *"Two hands and I can scale and spin it, like it's real."*

---

## 7. Throw it (15 seconds)

Grab the object and **flick your hand sideways quickly, then release**.

The object flies in the direction of your movement, arcs under gravity, bounces off the floor, and
rolls to a stop.

**Say:** *"Fast release — that's a throw. Gravity, bounce, the lot."*

---

## 8. Pause and reset (10 seconds)

**Open your palm** flat towards the camera. Interaction pauses.

Open your palm again to resume.

**Say:** *"Open palm pauses it, so you can hand over to the next person."*

---

## 9. The playground (optional, 30 seconds)

Switch to **Creative Playground**. A set of floating shapes appears.

Stack them. Arrange them. Throw them. Build a little scene.

**Say:** *"Everything you can see, you can touch."*

---

## 10. Wrap up

Press **Save snapshot** to give the visitor a PNG of their creation, or **Reset all** for the next
person.

---

## Operator cheat-sheet

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Cursor is shaky | Sensitivity slider left, or the **Relaxed** preset |
| Cursor feels laggy | Sensitivity right, or **Responsive** |
| Visitor's hand is too small on screen | Ask them to move closer |
| Visitor keeps accidentally grabbing | They are pinching without meaning to; ask them to keep the index finger extended |
| Tracking keeps dropping | Lighting. Point a lamp at their hand, not behind them |
| Too many objects on screen | **Clear canvas**, or **Reset all** |
| FPS dropping after a long session | **Reset all**, or reload the page |
| Kids' event | **Allow throwing** off; keep the gesture guide open |

## The 30-second version

If you only have half a minute:

> Draw a circle in the air → open your hand → pinch the sphere → move it → let go → flick it.

That is the whole product.
